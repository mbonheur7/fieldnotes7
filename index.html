A NOTE FROM THE MAKER

Campus life is full.

Campnotes is a small, browser-only planner for the lectures, study blocks, and campus moments that deserve a place in your week.

THE PURPOSE

Less juggling. More showing up.

When deadlines, reading, and the good stuff all compete for your attention, a simple place to put them can make the week feel more manageable. Your records stay in this browser unless you choose to export them.
